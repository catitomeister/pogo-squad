https://pokemongohub.net/post/guide/regional-pokemon-list/

Community-sourced. Other guides disagree on a few entries (Relicanth, Heracross, Kangaskhan), and events can open regionals
worldwide for a while. Verify before planning a trip. Saved 2026-10-23 for possible use in a future "Regional" page; not on the website.

## Multi-continent or hemisphere
- Europe, Asia, Australia: Volbeat, Zangoose, Sawk, Heatmor
- The Americas and Africa: Illumise, Seviper, Throh, Durant
- Europe, Middle East, Africa: Red Flabébé/Floette/Florges, Baile Oricorio, Diamond Furfrou, Curly Form Tatsugiri
- Europe, Middle East, Africa, India: Pansear, Simisear, Mesprit (raid / rare wild), Pheromosa (raid)
- The Americas and Greenland: Panpour, Simipour, Yellow Flabébé/Floette/Florges, Azelf (raid / rare wild), Buzzwole (raid)
- The Americas: Pom-Pom Oricorio, Debutante Furfrou, Droopy Form Tatsugiri
- Asia-Pacific: Pansage, Simisage, Blue Flabébé/Floette/Florges, Uxie (raid / rare wild), Xurkitree (raid), Sensu Oricorio, Star Furfrou, Stretchy Form Tatsugiri
- African, Asian, Pacific and Caribbean islands: Pa'u Oricorio
- Western Hemisphere: Lunatone, Blacephalon (raid), West Sea Shellos/Gastrodon, Blue Striped Basculin, Paldean Aqua Breed Tauros, Blue Plumage Squawkabilly
- Eastern Hemisphere: Solrock, Stakataka (raid), East Sea Shellos/Gastrodon, Red Striped Basculin, Paldean Blaze Breed Tauros, Green Plumage Squawkabilly

## By environment, not country
Wiglett (beach), Silicobra (desert-like), Toedscool (forest), Snowy Castform (snow land cover or Snow weather)

## Special cases
- Vivillon pattern follows the Postcard region; Fancy and Poké Ball patterns are not currently available.
- Furfrou trims: La Reine (France), Kabuki (Japan), Pharaoh (Egypt), via Form Change in that region.
- Flower Flabébé (White, Orange) and Plumage Squawkabilly (Yellow, White) are worldwide.
- Regional raid bosses only appear when they are in the current raid rotation.
